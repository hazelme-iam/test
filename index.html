HCI Design & Clinic System Architecture

1. Single-Column Path for Stress-Free Patient Intake Patients seeking healthcare need low-friction, distraction-free forms. The vertical single-column stacks inputs directly above the primary button, avoiding skipped fields.
2. Clean, Non-Repetitive Error Handling Zero duplicate hints. Inputs show bold labels and subtle placeholder examples. If an error occurs, the single informative `.error` message appears in high-contrast `#B42318` red and immediately disappears upon typing.
3. Non-Destructive Backward Navigation Patients can review their answers on Step 3 and safely navigate back to Step 1 or to the Account step without losing typed information.
4. Consequential Confirmation Verbs The final modal specifies the exact intake record ("Q3 roadmap") and consequence, providing clear "Cancel" and "Confirm" buttons instead of ambiguous "OK" prompts.
