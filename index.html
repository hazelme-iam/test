HCI Design & Clinic Flow Architecture

1. Structured Single-Column Hierarchy Users start with Authentication (Sign In or Register). After account verification, the single-column Stepper guides them linearly through personal, medical, and emergency sections without visual clutter.
2. Non-Destructive Stepper Navigation Progress bars clearly indicate completion (`Step 1 of 3`, `Step 2 of 3`, `Step 3 of 3`). Every step offers a "Back" button, allowing users to safely verify or edit previous fields without erasing entered data.
3. Pre-Submission Review & Explicit Confirmation Before final commitment, Phase 3 compiles an organized summary of all details. The confirmation dialog explicitly prompts "Confirm & Submit" or "Cancel", eliminating ambiguous generic "OK" prompts.
