HCI Design & Clinic Architecture Standards

1. Single-Column Hierarchy & Non-Distracting Layout Users start at Authentication and move linearly into the Information Stepper. No emojis or decorative distractions, ensuring a professional, clean medical environment.
2. Standard Philippine Mobile Phone Validation Phone input validates standard 11-digit Philippine mobile numbers starting with 09 (e.g. 0917 123 4567) or +639, with inline error feedback.
3. Non-Destructive Stepper Navigation & Review Progress bars display current position. Users can review all entered details in Phase 3 and click "Back to Edit" without erasing typed data.
